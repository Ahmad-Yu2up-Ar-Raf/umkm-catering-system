# Link / Visibility Prospects — Catering Bogor

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Status:** prospect list only — outreach NOT started
**Context:** low-authority market — most competitors rely on Google Maps + Instagram,
not real backlinks (landscape.md). Expect modest link value; local pack + content depth

## Prospect categories (targets to search when outreach starts)

1. **Bogor wedding & event blogs** — long-form articles listing vendors per area.
   Query: "vendor katering bogor", "rekomendasi katering pernikahan bogor",
   "catering untuk acara di bogor", "pastiin *blog* bogor wedding".
2. **Event organizer (EO) directories** — Bogor EO directories that list caterers.
   Query: "event organizer bogor", "vendor wedding bogor", "directory vendor bogor".
3. **Local food / kuliner blogs** — Bogor food blogs covering "masakan rumah",
   "nasi box", "tumpeng bogor", "snack box bogor".
4. **Venue sites** — wedding venues / gedung pertemuan in Bogor with vendor pages
   (cross-linking partner: they list our catering on their vendor page, we list their
   venue in our Galeri/Cara Pemesanan content).
5. **Local community / chamber sites** — Bogor UMKM directories, komunitas UMKM Bogor
   (relevant because Catering Nusantara is a UMKM with a real local story).
6. **Instagram cross-pollination** (not links, but a ranking signal lever):
   hashtags #cateringbogor, #cateringbogormurah, #paketpernikahanbogor,
   #nasi box bogor, #cateringweddingbogor (landscape.md) — consistent handle + posts.

## Rules

- **No fabricated URLs.** Prospect URLs are discovered via the search queries above at
  outreach time (this session is planning-only; do not guess links).
- **No paid links, no link farms, no spun guest posts** — Jagarasa's mini network is a
  known weakness, not a model.
- Every outreach touchpoint must offer real value (content contribution, venue
  cross-link, event coverage) — the "Down to Earth" brand extends to outreach.

## Priority order when outreach starts

1. Venue cross-links (highest relevance + conversion potential).
2. Bogor wedding/EO directories (listings — cheap, decent relevance).
3. Local food blog coverage (harder, higher value).
4. UMKM community mentions (trust signals).
