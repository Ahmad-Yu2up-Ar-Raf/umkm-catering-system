## Content Formats That Are Working

1. **Location landing pages** — Niezar `/catering-bogor/` (targeted, on-page SEO title/description, clear CTA)
2. **Long-form "catering pernikahan bogor" blog articles** — Jagarasa blog (WordPress), 20-min reads, Table of Contents
3. **Detailed package pages with menus + tiered pricing** — Jagarasa (Ruby/Saphire/Diamond with full menu lists), key ranking driver
4. **WhatsApp-first conversion** — every competitor leads with `wa.me` CTAs; Jagarasa even has `wa.jagarasa.id` subdomain
5. **Instagram hashtag engine** — Jagarasa uses #cateringbogor, #paketpernikahanbogor, #cateringweddingbogor, #cateringbogormurah (keyword + social cross-pollination)

## Backlink / Authority Observations

- Jagarasa runs a **mini link network**: cateringbogor.or.id + jagarasa.com + blog subdomain + order/pl/wa subdomains
- Most competitors rely on **Google Maps + social (Instagram)** rather than real backlinks — low authority market
- Niezar's strength = Google reviews (4.9★, 3rb events), not links
- **Implication:** Local pack + content depth can outrank weak domains without aggressive link building
