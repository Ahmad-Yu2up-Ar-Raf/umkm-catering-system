## Why this page
Nasi box is the core revenue product and the most-typed query family. The SERP is
held by price players (azzahra Rp12k, annasya Rp15k) and Niezar (Rp20k). **We do not
price-war** — position on "masakan rumah" quality + transparent pricing + reliability
(kapasitas 20–1000 porsi). If the client has a signature menu (is_best_seller),
make it the hero — the market rewards a "best seller" anchor (pricing-intel.md §Gap 2).
