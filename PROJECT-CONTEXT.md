# Catering Nusantara — Brand & Business Context

**Client:** Dapur Bunda Catering, Bogor — established 2019 (PIC: Ratna Kusuma)
**Positioning:** "Down to Earth" — honest home-cooked Nusantara flavors, not fine-dining. Real photos, no stock imagery.
**Brand colors:** Warm charcoal-black + cream + pure white (see attached logo.png).
**Design reference:** suasana.vercel.app (overall visual tone)
**Primary conversion channel:** WhatsApp ordering.
**Visual reference standard:** Dapur Solo's lunchbox photography style (attached) — bright, clean, high-key, pure-white background.
**Current problem:** Existing product photos have inconsistent backgrounds (sage green, yellow, beige). This project unifies all 5 products into one consistent photoshoot matching the Dapur Solo reference.
